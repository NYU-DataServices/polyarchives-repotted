About Me

Mahdi Chowdhury

Hi! My name is Mahdi Chowdhury and I am a rising junior at the New York University Tandon School of Engineering majoring in Computer Science and minoring in Finance. I chose to compile this website because it is my belief that everyone deserves to see themselves in the shoes that they want to one day occupy. I also want to highlight the achievements and adversities of Muslims during and after their time at Tandon. I hope that as a visitor, you are able to walk away with knowing a little more about the impacts as well as the experiences of the Muslim community on not just Tandon's history, but the history of our nation.
