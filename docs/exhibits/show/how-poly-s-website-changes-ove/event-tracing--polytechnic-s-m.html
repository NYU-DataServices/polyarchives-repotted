Event tracing: Polytechnic's Merger with NYU

"Exploring the Future: The Possible Merger of New York University and Polytechnic University."

 Merger Central

On August 7, 2007, Polytechnic's website featured a news article titled "Exploring the Future: The Possible Merger of New York University and Polytechnic University." This announcement, intended for all members of Polytechnic, introduced the potential merger between Polytechnic University and New York University.

The news article was promptly pinned to the top of the website's news section,. Additionally, a dedicated webpage called "Merger Central" was swiftly created to facilitate discussions among Polytechnic members regarding the merger. This central hub included crucial announcements about the merger's progress, related news updates, and a forum link for Polytechnic students, faculty, and staff to join and engage in discussions.

Remarkably, Merger Central remained active for an extended period, even after Polytechnic officially became affiliated with NYU, underscoring the significance and ongoing impact of this transformative event on the institution's community.

NYU-POLY

NYU-POLY

In April 2008, Polytechnic University released the "Overview of the Polytechnic University - New York University Affiliation and Proposed Consolidation Agreement," marking the official beginning of Polytechnic's merger process with NYU. This overview outlined the initial steps of Polytechnic becoming an affiliation of NYU and being renamed as the Polytechnic Institute of NYU.

Prior to September 16, 2008, significant changes were made to Polytechnic's website to reflect this affiliation and renaming. The website's icon was completely replaced by the NYU-Poly logo, signifying the new institutional identity. Furthermore, the title of the webpage was updated to ":: Polytechnic Institute of NYU :: New York's major educational resource in science and technology," aligning with the updated branding and mission of the institution under NYU's umbrella.

New NYU-Poly's website at 2014.1.23

At the beginning of 2014, Polytechnic Institution officially merged with NYU and was renamed to "NYU Polytechnic School of Engineering". To commemorate this significant milestone, a brand-new website was launched with notable changes that reflected the merger.

One of the most prominent changes was the appearance of the NYU icon at the website header, now bearing the name "POLYTECHNIC SCHOOL OF ENGINEERING," showcasing the integration of Polytechnic into NYU's academic framework.

Additionally, a prominent feature on the website was a large celebratory poster displayed prominently in the middle of the webpage, symbolizing the official merger with NYU and highlighting the shared vision and mission of the newly formed NYU Polytechnic School of Engineering.

Tandon's main page at 2015.10.06

In late 2015, the Polytechnic School of Engineering received a significant donation of $100 million from a couple named Tandon, resulting in the school being renamed to the Tandon School of Engineering. This transformative event was promptly reflected on the school's website.

On October 6, 2015, the Tandon School of Engineering updated its website icon, replacing the previous "Polytechnic School of Engineering" branding with the new name "Tandon School of Engineering." This change visually represented the school's acknowledgment and gratitude for the generous donation and highlighted the new identity bestowed upon the institution.
