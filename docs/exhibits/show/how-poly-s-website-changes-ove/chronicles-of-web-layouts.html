Chronicles of Web Layouts - Before 2005

poly.edu mainpage layout at 1997.4.27

The first tracable version of Polytechnic Institution's website in April 1997 explains simplicity. Although we already lost the access to the original images on the website, the fundamental structure of the webpage remains discernible. Each element is neatly arranged within tables, presenting a simple and straightforward layout devoid of embellishments. The content consists solely of text, with links to other pages. In this rudimentary form of website, there are no graphical elements, animations, interactive features or even color to be found. The website is a perfect example of Web 1.0(Duschenko, 2018), which is characterized by static content and a primary focus on information dissemination.

poly.edu mainpage layout at 1997.12.12

By the end of 1997, the website underwent its next evolutionary leap. A comprehensive restructuring of the website introduced the integration of more images and a denser layout. While the core elements remained largely unchanged, the addition of images was executed and arranged in a refined and aesthetically pleasing manner. This strategic enhancement not only enriched the visual appeal of the website but also contributed to a more engaging user experience as user could navigate through the site by clicking on captivating images rather than plain text links.

poly.edu PAT page at 1999.11.12

poly.edu PAT page at 1999.11.12

After years of relative stability, a significant update of Polytechnic Institution's website took place at the end of 1999. This update introduced a completely new site with a dramatic shift in its appearance and functionality. This iteration was characterized by an abundance of rich shapes and images, marking a departure from the previous minimalist design. The website now featured with an aesthetically pleasing graphical user interface (GUI) and adorned with captivating shapes, images, and a vibrant color palette. This visually appealing update represented a notable leap forward in Poly's efforts to modernize its online presence. Some noteworthy elements in modern web design, such as carefully designed buttons, logos, and inline images, were integrated into the site. This version of the website stood as a testament to Poly's commitment to embracing contemporary design trends and providing users with an engaging and immersive online experience.

poly.edu PAT page at 2003.1.22

At the beginning of 2003, a subtle update was made to the website's main page, involving the rearrangement of existing elements rather than the introduction of new features.

poly.edu mainpage at 2004.6.9

poly.edu mainpage at 2004.6.9 on hover

In 2004, a significant milestone was reached with Polytechnic Institution celebrating its 150th Anniversary, marking a pivotal moment for the website. This occasion introduced a substantial and unprecedented update, transforming the site into a more visually captivating and interactive platform.


The updated website featured with an attractive image positioned prominently in the middle of the page. Surrounded by this image on the left was a dynamic menu, while the right side showcased school news and highlights.


Moreover, this version also introduced a huge amount of interactive elements, enhancing user engagement. For instance, when you hover the mouse over the menu, it would trigger a grey circle at the image's center, revealing a more detailed navigation menu. This innovative feature empowered visitors with greater control over their browsing experience with great aesthetics.


This iteration of the website marked a significant leap forward, and incorporated an incredibly amount of interactions and visual enhancements that brought the site to life, making it more dynamic and vivid than ever before.
