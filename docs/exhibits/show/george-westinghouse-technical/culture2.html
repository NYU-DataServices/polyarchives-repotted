School Culture

George Westinghouse School boasts a rich legacy deeply intertwined with the vibrant fabric of Brooklyn. Since its inception in 1919, Westinghouse has served as an opportunity, equipping students with the tools and skills to chart their paths to success. Beyond shaping countless talented engineers poised to make a difference in the world, Westinghouse stands as the alma mater to illustrious Brooklynites such as Joe Pignatano, The Notorious B.I.G., and Jay-Z, further solidifying its profound cultural impact on both the city and the global stage.

Student Statements

Interview with Abdul Hafeez Muhammad

Interview with Abdul Hafeez Muhammad

In a recent interview, Abdul Hafeez Muhammad, a former Westinghouse student from the 1980s, shared insights into his time at the school. Though his recounting of experiences is marked by shocking incidents of violence, it is delivered with a remarkable sense of nonchalance, almost as if such occurrences were routine during his tenure at Westinghouse.

Despite the apparent dangers of the early 1980s environment, Abdul emphasizes the school's positive aspects and valuable contributions to the community. While some may perceive the students as troubled or on a precarious path, Westinghouse provided vital opportunities for self-improvement and community upliftment.

Abdul's trajectory serves as a testament to Westinghouse's transformative potential. He shows that when the school is equipped with the resources it requires and operates at its fullest capacity, it can provide remarkable opportunities to students in need, empowering them with the tools to alter their circumstances.

Review of Westinghouse

Howard Skolnik, a graduate of Westinghouse, reclaims his time at the school via InsideSchools. He states that being at Westinghouse was "THE critical stepping stone in making my rewarding career possible." Howard further elaborates that Westinghouse was far from a "bed of roses," underscoring its challenges, including crime and danger within the school. However, he emphasizes that the teachers' dedicated efforts truly made a significant difference. He underscores how it propelled his career forward. While acknowledging its flaws then and now, he emphasizes that it is a beacon of opportunity where students can strive for personal growth and pursue meaningful endeavors.

Review of Westinghouse

A recent assessment of Westinghouse provided anonymously via InsideSchools, sheds light on the institution's ongoing challenges. The author notes an ongoing atmosphere of danger, highlighting incidents such as students making threats of violence and frequent altercations. Despite these concerns, the dedication and competence of the teaching staff emerge as a beacon of strength, as attested by multiple student testimonies. Yet a concerning trend has emerged in recent years, shown by the school's latest statements. The once revered faculty at Westinghouse is no longer its cornerstone. Allegations have surfaced, suggesting that the school's administration, including the principal and assistant principal, pressure teachers to pass students, regardless of their attendance and performance. The administration's motivation behind this new initiative appears to be aimed at bolstering graduation rates, thereby securing additional funding. If verified, these claims could shed light on the declining testing scores reported by the school, pinpointing the root issue of why Westinghouse is failing today. Considering that this school review is anonymous and recognizing the tendency of online reviews to be either overwhelmingly positive or negative, it's prudent to withhold complete trust in its contents until it undergoes verification.

Review of Westinghouse

To bolster the credibility of the anonymous review, we can reference a recent testimonial from Matt sourced through InsideSchools. Matt recounts instances where the school administration allegedly pressured teachers to graduate students despite not meeting all requirements. He emphasizes that teachers who resist face repercussions from the administration. While the accuracy of these claims remains uncertain, they contribute to the silent nature of the project. Notably, InsideSchools features over 30 reviews of Westinghouse, several of which affirm concerns regarding administrative misconduct and premature graduation. The abundance of these accounts suggests a degree of validity, although it is only partially confirmed. While some reports may exaggerate the situation, they all touch on the same issue and cry for change within the school.

What is the Culture?

My analysis, based on the valuable insights gathered from InsideSchools and the interview with Abdul Hafeez Muhammad, provides a comprehensive yet nuanced understanding of the school's culture. While understanding the school culture as a whole is complex, specific trends do emerge from the information available, offering glimpses into Westinghouse's challenges.

The school environment at Westinghouse is not just challenging but also alarming. It is rampant with threats to the students, including fights, shootings, and other crimes, which seem to be more prevalent here than in other schools. This lack of safety significantly hinders the learning process. Moreover, there are concerning reports about the teaching staff, with allegations of disengagement and unprofessional behavior. The administration's focus on metrics over the students' holistic success further exacerbates the situation, creating an atmosphere where academic achievement is not valued and graduation rates take precedence over all else.

Furthermore, there is a pattern where the administration's priorities are pushed onto the teachers, creating a climate where student shortcomings go unaddressed. While these claims are difficult to verify without a firsthand experience, they echo consistent themes of concern.

With the school being described as it is, it is clear that the Westinghouse does not offer a culture that promotes success and learning. Westinghouse's environment could be the clear and possibly the most significant factor in its decline. But how did it get to this point, and what, if anything, is being done to curb this hostile environment? The answer to this falls again into silence, for the information is not retrievable without being hands-on with the school.
